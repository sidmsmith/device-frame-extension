Device Frame - Chrome extension
===============================

Install
1. Extract this zip to a permanent folder OUTSIDE OneDrive, e.g.
   C:\Users\<your name>\device_frame_extension
   (OneDrive "online-only" files can't be read by Chrome.)
2. Check that manifest.json is directly inside that folder
   (not inside another device_frame_extension subfolder).
3. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the folder from step 1.
5. Pin "Device Frame" from the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar.

Use
Open the page to demo (e.g. WM Mobile from the WMS menu) and click the
Device Frame icon. A separate window opens with the page inside a device.
The bar above the device lets you change device or size, rotate, show a
status bar, change the background, reload, and take a screenshot (saved
to Downloads and copied to the clipboard). Close the window when done.
Alt+Shift+S copies a screenshot to the clipboard without saving a file.
To hide the bar: click the collapse button (far right). To show it again: double-click the
device frame, press Alt+Shift+H, or click the small tab at the top.

Update
Replace the folder's files with the new version, then click the reload
icon on the Device Frame card in chrome://extensions.
